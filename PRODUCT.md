# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal staff at the company who need a correctly formatted email signature. They arrive infrequently (onboarding, a role change, a rebrand), spend under a minute, and leave. Most are non-technical and will not touch HTML; they expect to pick a template, type their details, copy, and paste into Outlook or Gmail.

## Product Purpose

A single-page builder that turns a few typed fields into a paste-ready HTML email signature. It exists to eliminate hand-built signatures that break formatting, drift off-brand, or use the wrong logo. Success is one clean copy-paste with zero follow-up questions to IT.

## Brand Personality

Polished, calm, quietly confident. It should feel crafted rather than utilitarian: a pleasant 30-second task, not a form to endure. Voice is plain and instructional, never jargon-heavy.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards bristling with panels, toolbars, and settings.
- Cluttered signature-generator sites buried in ads, upsells, and dozens of style toggles.
- The hero-metric / identical-card-grid landing-page template.

## Design Principles

- **The preview is the product.** The live signature is the center of gravity; everything else is supporting chrome and should defer to it.
- **One obvious path.** Pick a template, fill fields, copy. No branching, no hidden modes.
- **Show, don't tell.** Editing happens directly on the preview, not in a separate form.
- **Quiet until it matters.** Color and motion stay restrained, reserved for state changes (focus, error, copied) that the user needs to notice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal certification target. Hold to reasonable defaults: legible contrast for body text, visible focus states, and the existing `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Keyboard editing (Tab between fields) already works and should stay intact.
