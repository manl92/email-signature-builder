Getting started

  1. 1
    Choose your company template

    Click a logo card at the top. Each brand has its own layout, logo, and social links already set up.

  2. 2
    Fill in your details

    Click directly on any underlined text in the signature preview to edit it: name, job title, phone, and email. Use Tab to jump between fields.

  3. 3
    Copy the signature

    Click Copy signature. The formatted HTML signature is copied to your clipboard, ready to paste.

  4. 4
    Add to Outlook

    In Outlook, go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures → New. Give it a name, click into the editor, and paste (Ctrl+V or ⌘V).

    Also works in Gmail: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new.
